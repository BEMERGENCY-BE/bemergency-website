WHO WE ARE

Introducing The BEMERGENCY Project

The BEMERGENCY Project is a project in development with a vision of helping animals in need throughout Belgium. Our goal is to build a responsible initiative focused on animal welfare, rescue and cooperation with experienced professionals.

OUR PURPOSE

What we stand for

🐾 Animal welfare

🚑 Rescue vision

Working toward a future where animals in need can get appropriate help.

🤝 Cooperation

Building relationships with animal shelters, veterinarians and relevant authorities.
